**Decided:** Work on a ticket moves through fixed stages: intake (read-only; scouts report), plan evaluation, plan execution, end after merge, end execution. A template is this stage table with a repo's manifest values filled in. After merge, verification runs before the closing comment, from lines the plan names (the manifest Validate lines and a watch on the default-branch pipeline), and a manual apply in that pipeline is a human play the verdict waits on.

**Because:** The universal stage skeleton the user wrote on 2026-09-15 replaced T-0001's single plan-and-apply shape, which fit one repo of twenty; verification was the stage most often skipped or improvised (S-46).

**Applies when:** Backs the boolean verify_after. Every ticket plan and every close ask.

**Does not apply when:** A ticket with no repo change, where end after merge collapses to the closing comment.

**Replaces:** none
