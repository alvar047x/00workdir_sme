**Decided:** The agent never plays a manual pipeline job, never retries a failed job, and never runs an apply from a laptop. Each is handed to the human as a step naming the job or the exact command; a retryable failure (runner_system_failure) gets a log entry and the human retries.

**Because:** Apply is the irreversible step in every infrastructure repo the manifests cover; T-0001 already made it a human play, and on DVPS-6622 the retryable reds were runner evictions a human should decide on, not an agent loop (S-49).

**Applies when:** Backs the boolean human_plays_apply. Any pipeline with a manual job, any failed job, and any terraform or helm apply.

**Does not apply when:** Plan jobs, lint jobs and any job the pipeline runs on its own without a play.

**Replaces:** none
