**Decided:** Read a repo's default and protected branches from its manifest (or the repo itself) before branching, targeting an MR, or reasoning about what is deployed. Never assume main.

**Because:** Of 20 repos, 11 default to main, 10 to platinum, terraform-aws-pexip-common to dev, and silvercloud-web and infra-central carry development or develop. Hardcoding main is wrong for half the estate and fails silently when reasoning.

**Applies when:** Any git operation or statement that names a base, target, or deployed branch.

**Does not apply when:** 00workdir (main, personal).

**Replaces:** none
